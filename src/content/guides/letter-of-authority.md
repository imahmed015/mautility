## What an LOA is

A Letter of Authority (LOA) is a short document you sign that lets us act on your behalf with your energy or water supplier. It's standard across the industry: without one, your supplier won't discuss your account with us.

## What it lets us do

- **Ask your current supplier for your account details** — your contract end date, current rates, meter details and how much you use.
- **Get quotes from other suppliers using your real details**, rather than estimates.
- **Speak to suppliers about your account** while we're working for you.

## What it doesn't do

- **It doesn't sign you up to anything.** You're not committing to switch, and nothing about your supply changes until you've seen the options and agreed to one.
- **It doesn't give us access to your bank account** or payment details.
- **It doesn't let us log in to your supplier account.** We never ask for passwords.

## What's on it

Usually: your name (and business name, if it's for a business), the address of each supply, the meter or supply numbers (MPAN for electricity, MPRN for gas), who it authorises — us — what it covers, and how long it lasts.

Read it before you sign. If anything is unclear, ask us.

## How long it lasts, and withdrawing it

An LOA is valid for the period stated on it. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing [admin@mautilitysolutions.co.uk](mailto:admin@mautilitysolutions.co.uk), and we'll stop contacting your supplier on your behalf.

## Why we ask for one before giving prices

Energy and water pricing depends on your usage, your meters and the market on the day. A figure given without your real details is a guess — which is why we don't do instant online quotes. The LOA is what lets us come back with options that are accurate and worth acting on.
